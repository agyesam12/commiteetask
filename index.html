PSP Electronic Fraud Incidents, 2024 vs 2025

Recorded by Payment Service Providers (mainly mobile money operators)

What the numbers tell us

  • ↑Fraud has migrated to mobile money. PSP cases rose 54% while case counts at traditional banks fell by 34%.
  • !Rural institutions are exposed. Rural & Community Banks recorded 51% of all cash suppression/theft cases among SDIs in 2025.
  • →The cause is structural. The BoG links the shift to high transaction volumes and low digital literacy among everyday users.

Source: Bank of Ghana 2025 Fraud Report. The figures are quoted from the committee's research brief. Verify them against the published report before public use.
