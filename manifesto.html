A Secure and Sovereign Digital Ghana

Every Ghanaian, from the grandmother receiving remittances in the village to the trader building a business online, deserves a digital economy they can trust. This chapter builds on our 2024 record with seven priorities. Together they protect citizens, create jobs for our youth, and keep Ghana's critical data and infrastructure under Ghanaian control.
