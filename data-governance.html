7 · Open Questions

What I need to clarify with the Sub-Committee

  1. What format and deadline does the Sub-Committee want for decisions and notes? Is this register, completed, the expected submission?
  2. Will the Sub-Committee accept a joint position with the Cyber Sub-Committee on the four shared records, using the split proposed in section 5?
  3. Which member leads drafting for each pillar? Pillar A is led with the Presidency, NITA, NIA, Civil Service and Attorney General; Pillar B jointly with Finance and the Bank of Ghana; Pillar C jointly with CSA and security agencies.
  4. What is the Party's stance on the current government's draft Data Protection Bill and Cybersecurity (Amendment) Bill? Our positions on 01.14 and 11.07 depend on it.
  5. How should we handle the 2021–23 SIM registration audit findings, which occurred under the previous NPP administration? I suggest acknowledging the lesson openly and leading with the fix.
