Tensions to resolve before sign-off

  1. Markets First / Innovation Through Freedom vs mandates. Data-localisation rules (Priority 1) and procurement quotas (Priority 6) can raise costs or limit choice. Options: tiered, risk-based localisation (Decision Question 2), and time-bound, performance-based preferences for Ghanaian firms instead of fixed quotas.
  2. Smaller, Faster, Smarter Government vs new institutions. A forensics laboratory, cyber courts and sovereign cloud could read as government growth. Option: house them within existing institutions (CSA, Judiciary, existing data centres) and show the efficiency gains.
